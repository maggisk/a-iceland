# Gangandi vegfarendur í forgangi á blönduðum stígum

**Lýsing:** Frétt Reykjavíkurborgar (maí 2020) um lagalegt umhverfi gangandi vegfarenda og hjólandi á blönduðum stígum. Bætir flokk 3 (gangandi/virkir ferðamátar) sem var áður veikburða — sýnir hvernig stígakerfið er skipulagt og hvaða markmið eru um aðgreiningu hjóla og gangandi.

## Tilvitnanir til notkunar

- "cyclists are legally obligated to yield to pedestrians" — kjarna-lagaregla á blönduðum stígum
- "doubling the system to return pedestrian paths to pedestrians" — markmið Hjólreiðaáætlunar
- "2+1 system" — gömul aðferð (2m gangandi + 1m hjólandi) sem nú er hætt
- "March and April showed significant year-over-year increases among cyclists and pedestrians" — Covid-áhrif vor 2020

## Greining

### Helstu fullyrðingar

- Lagalegur grunnur: 42. gr. nýju umferðarlaga (149/2019).
- Reykjavík hefur þrjár stíga-gerðir auk hefðbundinna gangstétta: gangstígar, hjólastígar, blandaðir stígar.
- Á blönduðum stígum hafa gangandi vegfarendur forgang — hjólandi verður að víkja, óháð staðsetningu gangandi.
- Þegar pláss er knappt eiga hjólandi að hringja bjöllu eða kalla kurteislega.
- Söguleg þróun: Borg notaði fyrr "2+1"-málningu (2 m fyrir gangandi, 1 m fyrir hjól á 3 m stígum) — nú hætt vegna aukinnar umferðar.
- Hjólreiðaáætlun Reykjavíkur: aðalmarkmið er að aðskilja hjólandi og gangandi á öllum aðalleiðum (tvöfalda kerfið).
- Nýjar leiðir nefndar: Klambratún til Miklubrautar, Elliðaárdal (yfir stíflu), Breiðholtsmegin, leið frá stíflubrú að borgamörkum Kópavogs.
- Marz/apríl 2020: stór hækkun gangandi og hjólandi (Covid-faraldur, samkomutakmörkun).

### Aðferð og umfang

Stutt frétt frá Umhverfis- og skipulagsdeild Reykjavíkurborgar. Hugsuð sem upplýsing til almennings, ekki ítarleg stefnumótandi greining.

## Fullt efni

### Key Legal Framework

The article references Iceland's traffic laws, specifically directing readers to "Section 42 of the new traffic laws" for detailed regulations governing pedestrians and cyclists. New traffic legislation (Act 149/2019) contains "considerable discussion about pedestrians and cyclists and which rules must be followed."

### Path System Categories

Reykjavík's path network comprises three types plus traditional sidewalks:
- Pedestrian paths
- Cycling paths
- Mixed pedestrian-cycling paths

### Priority Rules

On mixed paths, "cyclists are legally obligated to yield to pedestrians." This applies regardless of pedestrian location on the path. When insufficient space exists, "cyclists must always politely—with a bell or gentle call—alert others and give people opportunity to step aside if needed."

### Historical Context

Years ago, city authorities attempted separating traffic with painted lines using a "2+1 system" (two meters for pedestrians, one for cyclists) on three-meter-wide paths. This line "was once the city's solution to accommodate both modes on paths" but "is no longer appropriate as pedestrian and cycling traffic has increased significantly over the past decade."

### Reykjavík Cycling Plan Goals

A primary objective involves separating pedestrian and cycling traffic on all main routes in Reykjavík and eventually the entire capital region through "doubling the system" to "return pedestrian paths to pedestrians."

### Specific Locations Mentioned

- Klambratún to Miklubraut area
- Elliðaárdal (above dam) — experiencing tension from fast cycling
- Breiðholtsmegin — new cycling path planned
- Route from dam bridge to Kópavogs/Reykjavík border at Breiðholtsbraut

### Traffic Observations

March and April 2020 showed "significant year-over-year increases among cyclists and pedestrians" as people utilized public outdoor spaces during gatherings restrictions.
