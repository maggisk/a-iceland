## Fullt efni

### Project Overview
Borgarlína is a proposed Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) network in Iceland's capital region, described as "a new backbone of public transport." The system aims to upgrade existing road infrastructure with dedicated public transport lanes that could later convert to light rail.

### Planning & Timeline
- Planning began: 2015
- Current status (Nov 2025): No passenger service yet; first phase expected 2031
- Original completion target: 2025 (later revised to 2026)
- Delays cited: COVID-19 pandemic impact

### Cost Evolution
- 2019: 120 billion ISK capital-area transport agreement
- 2025: Full Borgarlína program estimated at 130.4 billion ISK (42% of wider 311 billion ISK investment plan)
- Spending through May 2025: 5.33 billion ISK booked

### Phase 1 Infrastructure
- Hamraborg-Miðborg tunnel: completion targeted 2026
- Ártúnshöfði-Miðborg tunnel: completion targeted 2027
- Fossvogur Bay bridge: scheduled mid-2028 completion (reserved for public transport, cyclists, pedestrians)

### Vehicle Specifications
18-meter electric articulated buses with:
- 125-passenger capacity
- Step-free access
- Planned frequency: hourly during normal operation; every 7-10 minutes during rush hour
