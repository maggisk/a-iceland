# Reykjavík Municipal Plan 2040 — yfirlit

**Lýsing:** Yfirlitssíða Reykjavíkurborgar (enska) um aðalskipulagið sem var samþykkt 2021/2022 og kom í stað AR2010-30. Frumheimild fyrir stefnu Reykjavíkur í samgöngumálum og skipulagi. Sjónarhorn A vegna þess að borgaryfirvöld hafa skýra stefnu sem styður Borgarlínu og fjölbreyttar samgöngur, en heimildin er staðreyndamiðuð.

## Tilvitnanir til notkunar

- "eco-friendly travel habits" — leiðarljós skipulagsins
- "a new public transportation tying the city and neighboring municipalities together" — opinber lýsing á CityLine/Borgarlínu
- "Adopted October 19, 2021, signed January 13, 2022, effective January 18, 2022" — dagsetningar

## Greining

### Helstu fullyrðingar

- Aðalskipulag Reykjavíkur 2040 kom í stað AR2010-30.
- Samþykkt af borgarstjórn 19. okt. 2021, undirritað 13. jan. 2022, tók gildi 18. jan. 2022.
- Stefnan leggur áherslu á umhverfisvænar ferðavenjur sem leiðarljós.
- Borgarlínan ("CityLine") staðfest sem hluti af skipulaginu og lýst sem "nýjar almenningssamgöngur sem tengja borgina og nágrannasveitarfélögin saman".
- Landnotkun og þéttleiki byggðar ákveðin í skipulaginu (íbúðasvæði, atvinnusvæði, götur og stígar, útivistarsvæði).

### Aðferð og umfang

Stutt yfirlitsíða á ensku. Aðalskipulagið sjálft er miklu lengra og dýpra skjal (sjá tilvísun á PDF AR2010-30 í WebSearch niðurstöðum).

### Tengingar við aðrar heimildir

- Bætir við H001 ([Stjórnarráðið](H001-samgongusattmali-stjornarradid.md)) með skipulagslegum grundvelli fyrir samgöngusáttmálanum.

## Fullt efni

### Planning Horizon & Adoption
The Reykjavík 2040 Municipal Plan replaced the previous AR2030 framework. It was approved by City Council on October 19, 2021, signed January 13, 2022, and became effective upon publication in the Official Journal on January 18, 2022.

### Transportation Strategy
The plan emphasizes "eco-friendly travel habits" as a guiding principle. It addresses which transportation modes to strengthen and promotes green transportation solutions. A confirmed change involves the CityLine, described as "a new public transportation tying the city and neighboring municipalities together."

### Sustainability & Community Principles
The plan aims to determine "the location of residential areas and business districts, new streets and paths, areas reserved for recreation, and areas to be protected." It balances growth accommodation with environmental quality and urban appeal.

### Land Use & Density
The framework establishes conditions for continued city growth while addressing residential development in established neighborhoods. Confirmed changes include modifications to individual buildings in open areas and land use permissions at various sites.

### Borgarlína (CityLine)
This public transit project appears as a confirmed change in the plan, linking the city with neighboring municipalities.

### Modal Split & Specific Targets
The webpage does not provide explicit modal split percentages or quantified sustainability targets.

### Söguleg tilvísun (úr WebSearch niðurstöðum, ekki úr þessari síðu beint)

- Hlutfall göngu/hjóls úr 21% í 30%
