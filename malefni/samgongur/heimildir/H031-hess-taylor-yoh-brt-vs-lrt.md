# Light Rail Lite or Cost-Effective Improvements to Bus Service? (Hess, Taylor, Yoh 2005)

**Lýsing:** Akademísk grein eftir Hess, Taylor og Yoh (2005) sem rannsakaði 14 BRT-kerfi í Norður-Ameríku. Mikilvæg fræðiheimild fyrir flokk 1 (almenningssamgöngur — fræði) og flokk 7 (alþjóðlegur samanburður). Gagnrýnir tilhneigingu BRT-verkefna að vaxa í "Light Rail Lite" sem ofreyrt og dýrt. Beint viðeigandi fyrir Borgarlínu sem er einmitt BRT en með "premium gold" hönnun.

## Tilvitnanir til notkunar

- "most BRT projects on the drawing boards are more elaborate, more expensive systems than many currently in service" — kjarna-niðurstaða
- "some of the most successful and popular new BRT systems are high-quality services operating in mixed traffic and implemented at relatively low cost" — andstæð dæmi (lite valkostur)
- "elaborate LRT-type improvements to lines and stations in one or a few corridors rather than less splashy improvements" — gagnrýni á tilhneigingu
- "light rail lite" — hugtakið sem höfundar smiðjuðu

## Greining

### Helstu fullyrðingar

- Höfundar: Daniel B. Hess, Brian D. Taylor, Allison C. Yoh (UCLA Institute of Transportation Studies).
- Útgáfa: 2005, Transportation Research Record nr. 1927, bls. 22–30.
- Rannsóknarsvið: 14 BRT-kerfi á áætlun eða nýopnuð í Norður-Ameríku.
- Kjarna-spurning: Hvort BRT eigi að vera (a) ódýrari valkostur við LRT (light rail transit), eða (b) ódýrari uppfærsla á hefðbundnum strætó.
- Lykil-niðurstaða: Flest BRT-verkefni á áætlun eru "more elaborate, more expensive" en BRT í rekstri — þau detta í "Light Rail Lite"-flokk.
- Andstæð sönnun: bestu og vinsælustu BRT-kerfi eru þau sem keyra í blandaðri umferð á góðum gæðum (ekki sérakreinar) með lágum kostnaði.
- Höfundar gagnrýna að BRT-verkefni vaxi í "elaborate LRT-type improvements" á einum eða fáum leiðum frekar en breiða þjónustubætur yfir net.

### Aðferð og umfang

Akademísk grein í virtu fagriti (TRR, peer-reviewed). Empíríska rannsókn á 14 verkefnum með kostnaðargreiningu og þjónustugreiningu. Hentar sem fræðilegur rammi en er frá 2005 — eldri en endurnýjuð BRT-bókmenntir.

### Abstract Summary

The research examines how varying Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) configurations affect implementation costs across North American cities. The authors identify two competing conceptualizations of BRT: as a high-speed rail alternative versus as an affordable upgrade to traditional bus service.

### Key Findings

The study evaluated 14 planned and recently opened BRT systems. A central discovery reveals that "most BRT projects on the drawing boards are more elaborate, more expensive systems than many currently in service."

The researchers found that while "some of the most successful and popular new BRT systems are high-quality services operating in mixed traffic and implemented at relatively low cost," most emerging projects prioritize "elaborate LRT-type improvements to lines and stations in one or a few corridors rather than less splashy improvements."

### Conclusions

The analysis characterizes most examined systems as "light rail lite," indicating a trend toward costly infrastructure enhancements rather than cost-effective service improvements across broader transit networks. The research reveals that BRT cost trajectories depend significantly on technological complexity and service feature scope.
